What the proposal changes

  • Reading rhythm: body 14.5px at 1.7 line height, 14px between blocks (today 14px at 1.55 and 10px).
  • Headings: three serif levels with clear steps (30, 23, 18) and more space above than below. h1 and h2 keep their hairline rule, as today. h4 is a bold sans line, h5 and h6 become the small bracket-style labels.
  • Links: thin accent underline that brightens on hover.
  • Lists: muted markers, nested lists tighter.
  • Quote: card background with an accent bar, so it reads as a quote and not as an indent.
  • Body text: a little grey (--text-dim, #c5c5c5) instead of --text. Bold, italic, headings and the first column stay white (--text-strong); quotes are one step greyer.
  • Inline code: accent background. The button at the top switches between a tinted accent (20% fill, light accent text, accent hairline) and a solid accent fill with black text.
  • Code blocks: a header bar with the language and an always visible Copy; horizontal scroll instead of wrapping so code keeps its shape; strings use a light tint of the accent.
  • Tables: no vertical lines; header as a small uppercase label; row hover instead of zebra.
  • Alerts, checkboxes, keys: kept, slightly more padding.
